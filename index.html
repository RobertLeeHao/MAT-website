01The problem

Many models.
No team.

You already pay for several AIs. Getting real work out of them is still your job — and it breaks in four places.

Problem A

You run the workflow.

You pick a model for every step, paste the context from one chat into the next and keep track of who said what.

Costs youyour time

Problem B

Nothing carries over.

Each model remembers only its own chats. Switch tools or hit a limit, and the context — and what you taught it — is gone.

Costs youthe context, at every switch

Problem C

One model does everything.

A strong model is slow and expensive on the easy steps. A cheap one gets the hard steps wrong. Either way you pay for it.

Costs youmoney, or quality

Problem D

Nobody checks the work.

A mistake in one step flows into the next. Finding it, and redoing what was built on it, is up to you.

Costs yourework

06How it worksAnswers A · D

Start to finish, in one chat.

  1. 01

    Ask in one chat.

    Ask MAT in plain words. A question gets its answer right there; a job gets a plan, with its time and cost next to one strong model’s.

  2. 02

    MAT staffs it — or doesn’t.

    One model when one is enough. When the work splits, MAT puts a crew on cheaper models, side by side, in a drawer beside the chat. Close it, and one line under the chat’s title keeps you posted.

  3. 03

    It asks only when it must.

    Every stage passes MAT’s check before the next one starts. When a decision is yours, MAT’s question takes the place of the input, and the crew waits until you choose.

  4. 04

    You get the result.

    MAT signs off the last stage and hands you the work, with its sources, the time it took and what it cost.

08PrivacyAnswers B

Your work never passes through us.

MAT has no account and no server of its own. It runs on your computer and calls the models you connect directly, with your own keys.

  1. No middleman

    No account. No MAT server.

    There’s nothing to sign up for and nowhere for us to keep your work. The only thing we hold is your waitlist email.

  2. Stays local

    It all stays on your computer.

    Chats, projects, the queue and what MAT learns are saved locally; finished work lands in Documents › MAT. Your keys sit in your system’s keychain.

  3. Need to know

    Each model sees only its step.

    A scout gets the pages it reads, not your history. The writer gets the findings, not your other chats.

  4. Locked

    Locked and encrypted, if you want.

    Turn it on in Settings › Security: Touch ID or your login password opens MAT, and your chats, memory and keys are encrypted with AES-256.

Honest note. Each step’s text goes to the provider whose model runs it, under that provider’s terms. For work that mustn’t leave your computer, connect a local model and MAT can run the whole job on your own machine, web searches aside.

12Questions

Questions, answered.

Q 01

Is MAT another AI model?

No.

MAT runs on the models you already have — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and others, plus models running on your own computer. It decides which one does each step, and checks the work.

Q 02

Which subscriptions can I connect?

The ones you have.

Your Claude and ChatGPT plans, API keys from the major providers, any OpenAI-compatible server, and local models on your computer. The same provider can be added twice, for a work and a personal account.

Q 03

Where does my data live?

Locally.

Your chats, projects and what MAT has learned are saved on your own computer, and finished work lands in Documents › MAT. There’s no MAT server in between, and each model sees only the work for its step — 08 Privacy.

Q 04

Is it always cheaper than one model?

No.

On a small job, one model is cheaper and faster, and MAT says so and runs it that way. The bigger the job, the more a crew of cheaper models saves.

Q 05

Do I have to manage the crew?

Not yet.

For now MAT staffs every crew for you: you talk to MAT, in one chat, and it asks only when a decision is yours. Next, you’ll be able to build crews of your own — the seats, their models and the checks — and share them (Coming soon · 03).

Q 06

When can I use it?

As seats open.

MAT is in a closed beta, free while it lasts. Join the waitlist and we'll send you an invite.

C·01About

One chat. A crew when it pays.

MAT is a desktop app that turns the AIs you already pay for into one team.

What it is

You talk to MAT. A question gets an answer; a job gets one model, or — when the work splits — a crew of models, each on the step it does best, with MAT planning the work and checking every stage before the next one starts.

What we believe

Process beats raw IQ. A good team of ordinary models, planned and checked, does better work for less than one strong model alone — on the jobs that split. On the ones that don’t, MAT says so and uses one model.

Where it runs

On your own computer. Your chats, projects and what MAT learns stay there, and your subscriptions stay yours: MAT doesn’t resell a model, it uses the ones you connect.

C·02Privacy

What stays, what goes.

The short version, for the beta. The full policy comes with the first public build.

On your computer
Your chats, projects and their files, the queue, and MAT’s memory. Finished work is saved in Documents › MAT. Deleting a chat or a project never touches the memory; only Settings › Memory does.
Your keys
Kept in your system’s keychain, used only to call the provider they belong to.
What leaves
Each step’s work goes to the model on that seat — to the provider you connected, under your account and that provider’s terms. A seat sees the work for its step, not your whole history.
The waitlist
We keep your email, the link you came through, who invited you and your answer to our one question — to send your invite and beta news, and nothing else. It’s stored with Cloudflare and mailed through Resend; it’s never sold or shared. Every email has a link that deletes all of it.
This page
No cookies and no ad trackers. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn’t identify you.
